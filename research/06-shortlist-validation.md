# Shortlist Validation — 15 Candidates Deep-Checked

- **Validated:** 2026-09-17, by the coordinating agent (not the mining agents), via fresh live searches per candidate: direct-competitor scan, pricing-floor mapping, load-bearing-claim verification, channel check, and kill-criteria re-test.
- **Inputs:** the 15 candidates advanced in [05-problem-database.md](05-problem-database.md). Evidence packs for each live in the industry files; this file only records what validation *added or changed*.

## The meta-finding (read this first)

**In 10 of 15 candidates, the gap the complaints pointed to has already been filled — mostly within the last 18 months.** Free or sub-$50 tools now exist for carrier vetting; four indie STR-permit trackers launched; a pharma-funded competitor gives prior-auth software away; ScalePad's ControlMap owns MSP compliance distribution; funded startups with billion-point data moats own candidate-fraud detection. Complaint mining is no longer a secret — the AI-tooling wave of 2024-2026 is converting Reddit pain into products fast.

The five survivors share one of three structural protections, which is the real lesson for picking a solo-builder wedge:

1. **The catalyst is younger than 12 months** and incumbents haven't caught up (ESA payment rails, Visa Core Rules April 2026).
2. **Incumbent pricing floors structurally exclude the small end** (Soraban's 150-return minimum, Paperless Parts' ~$18k/yr, Instrumentl's $299/mo) — the down-market segment is unprofitable for them to chase.
3. **The niche demands domain specificity** that horizontal tools can't cheaply bolt on (state-by-state ESA formats, restricted-fund grant accounting).

## Survivors (advance to top-picks briefs)

### 1. `education-P1` — ESA-proof invoicing and receivables for microschools/tutors — **ADVANCE, conviction HIGH**
- Competition found: CohortLedger (microschool-focused ESA compliance; young, positioning close to the wedge), GetESAPaid (broader provider-compliance layer, 18 states), Omella (payments). No funded gorilla; ClassWallet/Odyssey are the state rails, serve the state, and are publicly failing providers (41,000-request Arkansas backlog).
- Market check: Texas TEFA is funding 2026-27 as the largest per-student program in the country; universal-ESA states keep adding. Provider counts grow with each state.
- Whitespace adjustment: 4 → 3 (two seed-stage entrants validate demand without settling the category).
- Why it survives: catalyst < 12 months old per state; horizontal invoicing tools can't cheaply encode per-state documentation formats; buyers (microschool founders) are concentrated in founder networks and are functionally forced to solve this or refuse ESA families.

### 2. `manufacturing-industrial-P1` — single-process job-shop quoting under the Paperless Parts umbrella — **ADVANCE, conviction HIGH-MEDIUM**
- Competition found: Paperless Parts (category leader, reported ~$10k setup + $18k/yr, sales-gated), AMFG (AI estimating, mid-market), DigiFabster ($2k–50k/yr), Costimator (legacy licenses), MRPeasy ($49–149/user ERP module), Tempus Tools ($75/mo — laser/fab only).
- Key validation: Tempus proves the cheap self-serve wedge sells in one process niche (laser). The equivalent slot for 2.5-axis CNC milling/turning shops quoting from PDFs is not filled at the $75–150/mo self-serve tier.
- Whitespace adjustment: 3 → 2.5 (AI-quoting entrants are moving; window is real but not empty).
- Why it survives: pricing-floor exclusion (structural protection 2) plus a daily-frequency workflow with one identifiable buyer (owner-estimator) and an elite channel (Practical Machinist, r/machinists).

### 3. `personal-services-P2` — Visa-compliant deposit and no-show enforcement as a bolt-on — **ADVANCE, conviction HIGH-MEDIUM**
- Load-bearing claim VERIFIED against primary sources: Visa's own guaranteed-reservation documentation limits no-show-fee protection to lodging/rental merchant categories; salons/med-spas are excluded, so stored-card no-show fees fall to Dispute Condition 13.1/13.7. Correctly structured advance deposits with separate click-to-accept and labeled receipts are the defensible pattern (Visa dispute-management guidelines).
- Competition found: GlossGenius ships flat-2.6% deposits + card-on-file rules (chargeback protection only for card-present transactions); Vagaro gates deposits behind its Shopping Cart add-on (the exact complaint in the evidence); Square gates no-show protection behind paid plans. Nobody ships the compliance-grade flow as an add-on for shops that keep their existing booking stack.
- Whitespace adjustment: 4 → 2.5 (incumbents have adjacent features; the add-on positioning plus enforcement-log/dispute-packet is the surviving differentiation).
- Why it survives: catalyst < 12 months (Visa Core Rules ed. 2026-04-18), and switching booking platforms is exactly what these owners refuse to do — an add-on monetizes the refusal.

### 4. `accounting-P1` — tax-season document chaser with teeth, for sub-150-return firms — **ADVANCE, conviction MEDIUM-HIGH**
- Competition found (dense, layered): TaxDome ($700/user/yr on 3-year terms; setup pain documented), Canopy ($150/mo+), Financial Cents ($19/mo, generic reminders), SafeSend Gather (consolidated-1099 handling complaints in evidence), and Soraban — the AI-intake specialist — priced $15–30/return with 150–250-return minimums plus $1,000–4,500 onboarding.
- Key validation: Soraban's minimums and onboarding fee structurally exclude the solo/1-3-person firm. The SMS-first chaser with blocking states ("return can't enter prep queue until file complete") at ~$30-60/mo self-serve has no direct occupant below Soraban.
- Whitespace adjustment: stays ~2.5 — crowded category, empty niche.
- Why it survives: pricing-floor exclusion; elite channel (r/taxpros); hard seasonal urgency creating a natural Nov–Jan sales calendar.

### 5. `npo-solar-P1` — grant-award ledger and reporting deadlines on top of QBO — **ADVANCE, conviction MEDIUM-HIGH**
- Competition found: Instrumentl ($299–999/mo, discovery-first — the price umbrella), Foundant's GrantHub **sunset January 31, 2026** (displaced users actively migrating), small QBO-native tools exist and are young: GrantLink ($25–150/mo, built by a practitioner), 4granted ($79/mo), GrantPipe ($269/mo), Aplos ($79/mo, replaces QBO entirely).
- Whitespace adjustment: 3 → 2.5 (small entrants exist; none owns the restricted-fund spend-down + funder-deadline surface, and the GrantHub sunset is a dated migration event).
- Why it survives: domain specificity (restricted-fund accounting semantics), a displaced-user event, and a channel (r/nonprofit) where the exact spreadsheet workflow is described in recurring detail. Caveat honestly: nonprofit willingness-to-pay is modest — price to be charged against grant admin budgets.

## Cut after validation (pain confirmed, wedge contested)

### 6. `npo-solar-P6` — 2026-correct solar incentive/TPO quote engine — **CUT** (was the top raw score, 41.0)
Aurora and OpenSolar already shipped the fix: OpenSolar OS 3.9 added prepaid lease/PPA with 48E prepayment parameters; Aurora runs TPO financier integrations (GoodLeap, EnFin, LightReach, Participate) with eligibility-driven pricing. Meanwhile SEIA/Wood Mackenzie confirm a 21–23% residential contraction in 2026 with a major installer bankrupt — the buyers are distressed and the incumbents closed the gap first. Catalyst real, window already shut.

### 7. `agriculture-P1` — H-2A filing cockpit — **CUT**
Seso is a funded, full-stack H-2A software+service company (files ETA-790A/9142A/I-129, timeline tracking, audit files, onboarding), with Ganaz adjacent. The DIY-cheap slice under Seso exists in theory but competes against a vendor whose product includes the legal capacity a solo cannot carry. Whitespace 4 → 1.5.

### 8. `healthcare-private-practice-P1` — prior-auth copilot for small practices — **CUT**
SamaCare is free for practices (pharma-funded) for medical-benefit drug PAs; EasyPA sells at $50/user or $250/clinic unlimited with explicit CMS-0057-F positioning; TurboPA covers procedures/DME cheaply; ApprovalHelp at $499/mo. The exact SMB price points are occupied, one competitor's price is zero, and PHI custody still applies. Whitespace 3 → 1.5.

### 9. `insurance-P2` — commission reconciliation for non-Epic agencies — **CUT**
Commission Tracker ($67/mo + $187/mo done-for-you), CommissionsWizard ($23–79/mo, AI PDF parsing), and DB Commission (HawkSoft-certified, exactly the "shops Applied won't serve" wedge) already occupy the sub-$200 tier; EZLynx ships native commission accounting. Whitespace 3 → 1.5.

### 10. `logistics-trucking-P1` — solo-broker carrier vetting — **CUT**
The documented "any sub-$50 alternatives?" demand has been answered since the mining threads were written: DOTScreener (free, unlimited, timestamped Carrier Selection Records — the audit-PDF wedge verbatim), Carrier90 ($29/mo), Trucken ($20/mo), CarrierPacket.Link (from $0, PASS/WARN/FAIL risk score), CarrierShield ($10/mo). An indie gold rush is mid-swing; entering now is entering fifth. Whitespace 3 → 1.

### 11. `real-estate-property-management-P2` — STR compliance tracker — **CUT**
RentPermit ($19/mo), PermitGuard ($29/mo), HostReady (1,085 markets, registry verification, autopilot filing), Trackable Doors, Totmatic — four-plus direct indie competitors, several launched around the same Austin July 2026 enforcement wave that made this urgent. Whitespace 4 → 1.5.

### 12. `restaurants-hospitality-P1` — delivery-dispute evidence automation — **CUT**
Voosh, Detect Recover, Dispute Dojo (20% success fee), Lunchbox Dispute Manager, Otter Verify, PAR OPS, Orders.co, KoreFi. The camera-clip-matching angle is genuinely un-shipped, but the recovery workflow around it is a contested category with platform-integration players. Whitespace 4 → 1.5.

### 13. `hr-staffing-P1` — candidate-authenticity pre-screen — **CUT**
Tofu, Brainner, Endorsed, Crosschq, Sardine, Checkr, Incode, Mockinta — funded vendors with billions-of-datapoints moats and ATS integrations. Fraud detection is an adversarial data game a solo cannot win; the SMB tier will be reached by these vendors' self-serve plans before a solo can build the data. Whitespace 3 → 1.

### 14. `it-msp-P1`+`P2` — MSP compliance-evidence vault — **CUT**
ControlMap (ScalePad) is precisely this product: multi-tenant, $99–299/tenant/mo, 63 frameworks, automated evidence, cyber-insurance questionnaires, CMMC SSP/SPRS/POA&M — distributed through ScalePad's enormous MSP install base; Compliance Scorecard is the multi-tenant alternate. A sub-$99 micro-tier exists in theory but channel gravity belongs to ScalePad. Whitespace 3 → 1.5.

### 15. `creative-creator-economy-P3` — statute-backed freelance invoice enforcement — **CUT (watchlist)**
PayShield is an indie already shipping the exact wedge (statutory interest math, 5-stage chase, AI demand letters, free tier); Freelance Recovery ships the calculators; NYC DCWP provides free filing with double damages. The differentiated slice (US state-statute depth: NY FIF + CA FWPA-specific paper trails) remains partially open — keep on the watchlist if PayShield stays EU-centric, but it no longer clears the high-conviction bar.
