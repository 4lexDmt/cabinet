# Period maps — public domain

Interactive country polygons are Natural Earth Admin 0, 1:110m
(https://www.naturalearthdata.com). Natural Earth data is public domain.

Period charts are historical scans, not OSM tiles (OSM is ODbL).

Do not replace these with `aourednik/historical-basemaps` or other GPL/CC-BY-SA
sets. Those would copyleft the rest of the game.

Empire shapes on the political layer are modern Natural Earth countries grouped
to game territory ids. They are a playable stand-in, not a treatise on 1815 or
1914 borders. The period chart is the historical document.
